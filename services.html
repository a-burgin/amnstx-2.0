Services

Notary services for everyday, mobile, online, and business needs.

AMNS TX helps clients complete notarizations professionally, conveniently, and in compliance with Texas notary requirements.

Traditional Notary Services

For documents that require a notarial act such as an acknowledgment, jurat, oath or affirmation, certified copy where permitted, or general notarial certificate. Common requests include affidavits, powers of attorney, financial forms, school forms, vehicle transfer documents, business documents, and sworn statements.

Mobile Notary Services

We travel to approved homes, offices, hospitals, senior facilities, apartment communities, public meeting locations, and business locations across the Dallas–Fort Worth area.

Online Notary Services

Eligible documents may be notarized remotely through secure audio-video technology. Clients must complete identity verification, have camera access, and use a valid government-issued ID.

Business & Corporate Services

Recurring and one-time support for property management companies, law offices, real estate professionals, healthcare offices, title teams, and corporate clients.
