FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers before booking your mobile or online notary appointment.

What do I need for a notarization?

You need a complete document, valid government-issued photo ID, all required signers present, and any required witnesses if your document calls for them.

Can AMNS TX tell me what document I need?

No. We are not attorneys and cannot provide legal advice, choose documents, draft documents, or explain legal rights. Please contact the document issuer or an attorney.

Can you notarize a document with blank spaces?

Generally, documents should be complete before notarization. Required blank sections may prevent notarization.

What IDs are accepted?

A valid government-issued photo ID is typically required. Examples may include a state driver license, state ID, U.S. passport, military ID, or other acceptable government-issued identification.

Do you offer mobile notary services?

Yes. We provide mobile notary services throughout Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding areas.

Do you offer online notarization?

Yes. We provide secure remote online notarization for eligible documents statewide throughout Texas.

How much is online notarization?

Online notarization may include an online session fee plus applicable Texas notarial act fees and platform fees. Pricing is confirmed before booking.

Are same-day appointments available?

Same-day appointments may be available depending on scheduling, location, document eligibility, and signer readiness.
