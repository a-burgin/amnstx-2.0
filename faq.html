FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers before booking your mobile or online notary appointment.

Do you offer traditional walk-in notary service?

AMNS TX currently focuses on mobile notary and online notary appointments. Meeting at a coffee shop, office lobby, library, apartment community, or other public location is treated as a mobile appointment.

What do I need for a notarization?

You need a complete document, acceptable identification, all required signers present, and any required witnesses if your document calls for them.

Can AMNS TX tell me what document I need?

No. We are not attorneys and cannot provide legal advice, choose documents, draft documents, or explain legal rights. Please contact the document issuer or an attorney.

Can you notarize a document with blank spaces?

Documents should be complete before notarization. Required blank sections may prevent notarization.

What IDs are accepted?

Acceptable identification generally includes current government-issued photo identification or another identity method allowed by Texas law, such as personal knowledge or a credible witness when applicable.

What if I do not have acceptable ID?

Contact AMNS TX before booking. Texas law may allow identity to be established through a credible witness or personal knowledge in specific situations, but the notary cannot proceed without a legally acceptable identity method.

Do you offer online notarization?

Yes. We provide secure remote online notarization for eligible documents statewide throughout Texas.

Are same-day appointments available?

Same-day appointments may be available depending on scheduling, location, document eligibility, and signer readiness.
