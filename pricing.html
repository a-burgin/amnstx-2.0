Pricing

Clear pricing before your appointment.

AMNS TX uses Texas state-regulated notary fees plus any applicable mobile travel, convenience, or online platform/session fees.

Traditional Notary

$10 first signature for an acknowledgment/proof, plus $1 each additional signature on the same certificate when applicable.

Oath / Jurat

$10 for administering an oath or affirmation with certificate and seal, or other notarial act as allowed by Texas law.

Online Notary

Online notarization may include up to a $25 online notary fee plus applicable Texas notarial act fees and any platform fees.

Mobile Notary

Mobile service includes Texas notary fees plus travel/convenience fees based on distance, timing, location, and appointment details.
