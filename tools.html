No acceptable ID?

If a signer does not have acceptable ID, the document may still be notarized only if Texas law allows another identity method, such as the signer being personally known to the notary or being identified by a credible witness. A credible witness must be able to swear or affirm the signer’s identity and must meet applicable requirements.

If you do not have acceptable ID, text AMNS TX before booking. We can explain appointment options, but we cannot bend identification rules or notarize without legally acceptable identification.
